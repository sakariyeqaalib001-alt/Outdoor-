## Part B — Business decisions (recommended defaults)

### B1 — Customer identity & duplicates

**D-01 Identity model** *(blocks Sales, Retention, Audit)*
- One **customer** record per real customer; **many sales** may link to one customer over time.
- Normalized phone and Index Number are each **globally unique** across all customer records, including expired customers.
- **New sale:** phone and Index must *not* already exist. If either exists → submission blocked, **duplicate attempt** event logged, Super Admin notified.
- **Retention sale:** links to the existing customer if found. If not found (customer predates the system), the agent creates the customer record and the sale goes to Team Leader approval as normal (already required).
- A Retention submission whose phone matches one customer but Index matches a different customer (or names materially differ) → blocked + duplicate attempt event.
- A customer cannot have two non-reversed sales in the same month (prevents double registration of the same retention).
- Changing a customer's phone or Index is a Super Admin action with reason; old value kept in history and still reserved.

**D-02 Index Number — INPUT NEEDED**
What is it (service account no., installation index, ONT ID?), who issues it, format/length, and can it be reused? Until answered: stored as trimmed, upper-case text, unique, format validation configurable.

**D-03 Phone normalization**
Stored in E.164 form (`+252…`); agents may type with or without `+252`/leading `0`; display form kept. Allowed Somaliland mobile prefixes configurable in Settings.

### B2 — Sales, products and eligibility

**D-04 Product catalog — INPUT NEEDED (price list)**
- Products: **FTTA, FTTH, FTTB, LTE** (payroll unit columns).
- Price is **chosen from the catalog price list** (with effective dates), not typed by the agent. Price snapshot stored on the sale.
- **1 sale = 1 unit.**
- Needed: the actual price list per product (and per city if different).

**D-05 Permanent-agent pay**
Payroll module calculates **incentive only** for permanent staff; Base Salary shown as $0 / "paid via HR payroll". Temporary agents: $100 base.

**D-06 Eligible sale (one definition used by targets, dashboard "approved" figures, payroll and rankings)**
A sale is eligible when all are true:
1. Status **Approved** (New sales are approved automatically when they pass validation; Retention after Team Leader approval).
2. Not reversed by an approved refund.
3. Not linked to an audit case in **Confirmed Issue** state.
4. Not a product switch (switches are never sales).
5. Counted in the **month of submission** (business timezone), provided it is approved before the **payroll cutoff** (default: 3rd day of following month, configurable). Approved after cutoff → counts in the month approved.

Sales linked to an **Open / Under Review** audit case: counted but the agent's payroll line is **held** (cannot be finalized) until the case is closed.

**D-07 Incentive scope**
`MAX(0, eligible units − 15) × $5`, per agent per month, **all products combined**, same rule for temporary and permanent agents.

**D-08 Base salary pro-rating — INPUT NEEDED (Finance)**
Recommended default: temporary-agent $100 is pro-rated by calendar days active in the month (start date, deactivation date); approved leave does **not** reduce it. Finance to confirm.

**D-09 Revenue & free-month**
- Revenue = **amount collected at registration** (price snapshot × months paid).
- Free-month compensation: a payment option on the sales form ("Pay 2 months, get 1 free"); qualifying amount configurable (default **$50**); sale flagged `free_month = true`; revenue = amount collected ($50); counts as **1 unit**.

### B3 — Stock

**D-10 Stock item names**
Canonical items: **WiFi Router, BDCOM, CALIX** ("BDCOM" is the equipment vendor name; "PDCOM/BPDCOM" treated as typos). Display names editable in Settings. **Confirm.**

**D-11 Custody chain**
- Locations: **Central Store (Stock Officer) → Branch → Agent custody**.
- Stock Officer records receipts into Central Store and transfers to branches/agents; agent acknowledges receipt.
- A sale deducts from the **submitting agent's custody** balance; negative balance blocked.
- Returns (refund, switch, faulty) move stock back to Branch/Central with reason.
- Opening balances loaded once via the legacy import (D-21).

**D-12 Serial numbers**
Routers and terminals (WiFi Router, BDCOM, CALIX) are **serial-tracked**: serial captured at receipt, at transfer, and selected at sale. A serial can be issued to only one active customer. Strongest anti-fraud control for equipment. Bulk serial entry by scan/paste.

### B4 — Workflows

**D-13 Audit case states**
Open → Under Review → Confirmed Issue / False Positive → Resolved. (BRD list kept; SDD's "Inconclusive" dropped — use Under Review with a note.) Only Super Admin and assigned Auditor can change state; no one can delete a case or an audit event.

**D-14 Retention approval**
Approver = Team Leader/Supervisor of the agent's team; Super Admin may approve as fallback. **No self-approval.** Rejection requires a reason. Stock for a Retention sale is deducted at **submission** (equipment physically leaves custody); if rejected, a return movement is required.

**D-15 Refunds**
- Any agent/Team Leader may submit; only Super Admin approves.
- Approved before payroll finalization → sale excluded from units, revenue and incentive for that month.
- Approved after finalization → negative adjustment line in the next payroll run; finalized payroll is never edited.
- Returned equipment recorded as a stock return movement.

**D-16 Product switch**
FTTA ↔ FTTH only; same-product switch blocked. Never counted as a sale, unit or incentive. New equipment issued from agent custody; old equipment returned. Identity (name, phone, Index) must match existing customer.

**D-17 Follow-up**
- Customer whose sale is in month M is due for follow-up during month M+1 (New and Retention).
- Outcomes: **Active**, **Expire**, plus **Not Reached** (tracked separately so reports don't treat unknowns as Active or Expire).
- Not completed by end of M+1 → stays "Not Reviewed" and appears on an overdue list.

**D-18 Leave**
- Agents submit their own requests; Super Admin approves/rejects; Super Admin assigns annual entitlement.
- Signed approval document attached after approval (PDF/JPG/PNG, max 5 MB, private).
- Days counted as **working days excluding Fridays and configured public holidays**. **Confirm with HR.**

**D-19 Rankings — confirm wording**
Monthly, by eligible units: **#1 Champion, #2 Top Sales, #3 Hall of Fame**? (BRD gives the titles but not the order.) Tie-break: higher eligible revenue, then earlier time the final unit was approved. Agents with a Confirmed Issue that month are excluded.

### B5 — Platform, access and operations

**D-20 Authentication**
- Permanent users: email + password (Supabase Auth).
- Temporary agents: phone + password; account created by Super Admin with phone pre-verified; system-generated temporary password; forced change at first login; **OTP by SMS** at sign-in through the Supabase Send-SMS hook to a local SMS provider.
- 5 failed attempts → 15-min lockout; OTP valid 5 min, max 3 resends per 15 min.
- **Pre-build spike required** to prove this works with a provider that reaches Somaliland numbers. **SMS provider: INPUT NEEDED.**

**D-21 Legacy data import (new)**

Import runs on staging first with a data-quality report (existing duplicates resolved by business before load). **Availability/format of legacy data: INPUT NEEDED.**

**D-23 Notifications**
In-app + email to Super Admin for duplicate attempts and audit cases; in-app for approvals waiting; SMS **only** for OTP (no customer data in SMS).

**D-24 Localization**
Timezone **EAT (UTC+3)**; currency **USD**, 2 decimals; date `DD/MM/YYYY`; UI **English for v1**, built with translation files so **Somali** can be added without code changes.

**D-25 Device approach**
Responsive **installable web app (PWA)**; no native app; no full offline mode in v1. Sales form autosaves drafts locally and submission is idempotent (safe to retry on poor connection). GPS/photo evidence deferred to Release 2.
