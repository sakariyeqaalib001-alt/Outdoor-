# Team Leader / Outdoor Supervisor guide

## Every day
1. **Approvals**: approve or reject every waiting sale of your team. Check name, phone, Index Number, product, plan, months and amount.
2. When approving, record the **device you hand over** — exactly one: WiFi Router for FTTA; PDCOM or CALIX for FTTH / FTTB; none for LTE. It is taken from your team's stock at that moment; if your stock is short the approval is refused.
3. **Reject** with a clear reason when something is wrong; the agent submits a corrected sale. A rejected New sale frees the customer's phone and Index.
4. Approve before the **3rd of the next month** so last month's sales still count in last month.

## Stock
- When the Stock Officer sends devices, open **Stock** and press **Accept** only when they have physically arrived.
- Your team's stock can never go below zero. Ask the Stock Officer for more before you run out.

## Spotting a returning customer registered as New
A customer who already had Sogasho must be **Retention**, not New. Be suspicious when:
- the system blocked a New sale for the same phone or Index and the agent then tries different numbers;
- the name, area and plan match an existing customer (Customers → search);
- one agent has many New sales in a short time, or several agents try the same customer.
Reject the sale and explain; every blocked attempt is already recorded for the Super Admin and Auditor.

## Your own sales
You may register and approve your own sales (D-40). Your own product switches and refunds need the Super Admin. You cannot sell in another city.

## Refunds
You may request a refund for any approved sale of your team (your own included): open the sale → **Request refund**. Only the Super Admin approves or rejects; you are notified either way. An approved refund marks the sale **Refunded** — it stops counting for the team. Make sure the device goes back to the Stock Officer.

## Your dashboard
**Dashboard → Branch Performance** shows your team: each agent's sales this month against the target of 20, and the band (Target met ≥ 20, Baseline 15–19, Underperforming below 15). **Operations** shows your team's devices issued and sales waiting for approval.
