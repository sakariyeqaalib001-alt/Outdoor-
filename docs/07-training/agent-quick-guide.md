# Sales Agent quick guide

## First sign-in
1. Open the app address your Team Leader gives you. On Android: Chrome menu → **Add to Home screen**.
2. Enter your **phone number** and the **temporary password** you were given.
3. Choose your own password (keep it secret — nobody from Sogasho will ever ask for it).
4. Enter the **6-digit SMS code**. You need a new code each time you sign in.

## Registering a sale (Sales → New sale)
1. **New** = a customer who has never had Sogasho before. **Retention** = an existing customer renewing.
2. Enter the customer's **name**, **phone** (063 or 065) and **Index Number — exactly 8 digits**, copied carefully from the customer's document. Check every digit before you continue.
3. Choose the **product** (FTTA, FTTH, FTTB, LTE) and **plan**, the **months paid** (1–12) and the **branch** where you will hand in the money.
4. Check the **amount** shown — this is what you must collect.
5. Press **Submit once**. If the network is slow, wait — pressing again will not create a second sale.

There is no date field: the system records the time itself. A sale submitted after midnight on the last day counts in the new month.

## After you submit
- The sale is **Waiting for approval** until your Team Leader approves it and gives the customer the device (WiFi Router for FTTA; PDCOM or CALIX for FTTH / FTTB; nothing for LTE).
- Only **approved** sales count towards your target and pay.
- **My Sales** shows this month; past months are read-only.

## If the system says no
| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| Phone / Index already registered | This customer exists. Do **not** try another phone or Index — that is recorded and checked. Ask your Team Leader; it is probably a **Retention** |
| Already has a sale this month | One sale per customer per month |
| Index must be 8 digits | Check the document again |

## Your dashboard
**Dashboard → Sales Overview** shows your own sales: this month, overall, New and Retention. Target: **20 eligible sales a month** (Target met); 15–19 is Baseline.
